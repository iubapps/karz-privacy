KARZ

Privacy Policy

Vehicle management, with privacy in mind.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

KARZ is a vehicle management application developed by IUB Apps. This policy describes how KARZ handles information when you use its vehicle tracking, Secure Document Vault, optional Google Sign-In, and backup and restore features.

1. Information You Choose to Store

You may enter vehicle details, odometer readings, fuel and maintenance history, repairs, expenses, service reminders, workshop notes, and other vehicle-related records. You may also select photos, receipts, PDFs, or other supported documents. Some documents may contain personal information you choose to include.

KARZ does not require a Google account to use its core local vehicle-tracking features.

2. Local Storage and SQLite

KARZ stores its main Garage and vehicle-management data locally on your device using SQLite and associated application storage. Supported core functions can operate without an internet connection. Local records are not automatically published or sent to an IUB Apps-operated server.

Your local data may be affected by device loss, app-data deletion, uninstallation, operating-system behavior, or device-level backup settings. We recommend keeping a separate backup of important information.

3. Secure Document Vault

Secure Document Vault lets you keep selected personal and vehicle documents in KARZ. Imported Vault files and their protected index are encrypted in the app's private device storage. Vault access uses supported Android device authentication, such as a configured screen lock or biometrics, and includes optional automatic-lock settings.

Vault documents are separate from ordinary Garage backups. Creating a regular KARZ backup does not automatically include Secure Vault documents.

Device authentication and encryption reduce exposure, but cannot guarantee protection against every device compromise, software vulnerability, or loss of access. A device screen lock is required for the current Vault access experience.

4. Optional Google Account Connection

Connecting a Google account is optional and is used for Google Drive backup and restore functions. KARZ uses Google Sign-In and receives authorization for the Drive functions you approve. The app may use your Google account email address locally to identify or remember the connected backup account.

KARZ does not create a separate IUB Apps online account or require an IUB Apps password to use its local features. Google's authentication and service operations are also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Google Drive Backups

If you choose Google Drive backup, KARZ transfers the relevant backup data to private application-specific storage associated with your Google Drive account. Ordinary Garage backups can contain vehicle records, expenses and supported attachments. A separate, passphrase-protected backup mechanism is available for Secure Document Vault documents.

The managed Vault Drive backup feature retains a latest encrypted backup and a previous recovery copy, subject to successful operations. A Vault recovery passphrase is required to decrypt a portable Vault archive; KARZ does not store that recovery passphrase in Google Drive as part of the managed backup.

Google Drive backups require network access and are subject to your Google account's available storage, permissions and Google's service terms. KARZ does not operate its own remote storage server for these backups. You control whether to use the backup features and which Google account you connect.

6. Local Exports, Sharing, and Recovery

KARZ provides backup/export options that can send files through Android's sharing interface to a destination you choose, such as another application, cloud storage or a local file destination where supported. Once you export or share a file, the receiving application, recipient and storage location may have their own privacy and security practices.

Ordinary backups and encrypted Vault archives have different security characteristics. Keep exported backups in a safe location, protect any recovery passphrases, and avoid sharing private backup files with untrusted parties.

Some upgrades from older KARZ storage formats may require manually exporting a backup and restoring it into the newer SQLite-based Garage rather than automatically transferring legacy records. Follow any in-app migration instructions and verify a usable backup before choosing to start fresh.

7. Notifications and Device Permissions

With your permission, KARZ may display maintenance or service notifications. It also uses supported Android authentication and file-selection functionality when you choose to access Secure Vault or import documents. You can manage relevant permissions through Android Settings.

8. Third-Party Processing and Data Sharing

KARZ uses Google authentication and Google Drive services for optional backup operations. These providers may process account, network or technical information needed to deliver their services. Android and Google Play may also process diagnostic and security information as part of the platform's normal operation.

We do not use vehicle records or Vault documents to sell personal information or display behaviorally targeted advertisements in KARZ. We do not intentionally make your records public. Data is transmitted to Google when you activate supported Google services, or to destinations you select when sharing or exporting data.

9. Retention, Deletion and Access

You can edit or delete supported Garage records and Vault documents within KARZ, and you can remove local app data using available reset controls or Android's app storage settings. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app may permanently erase local information that has not been backed up.

Local deletion does not automatically erase external exports, files shared with other apps, or backups previously saved in Google Drive. KARZ includes controls for managing supported application backups; you may also need to manage retained files in the relevant service or location. A managed encrypted Vault backup may contain documents that were later deleted from the live Vault.

KARZ does not maintain a separate IUB Apps user account for its current core functionality. If you have a privacy or data-handling question, contact us using the address below.

10. Security and Limitations

We use local app storage, Android authentication, encryption for Secure Vault documents and passphrase-protected Vault export archives, and secure connections to Google services where applicable. Ordinary Garage backups are separate from the Vault's passphrase-protected archive format and should not be assumed to have identical encryption protections.

No security control can guarantee absolute protection. Protect your device, Google account, exported backups and recovery passphrases. Avoid storing important information exclusively in one place.

11. Children's Privacy

KARZ is intended for general vehicle management and is not designed specifically for children. It is intended for people managing vehicles, maintenance and related expenses.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as KARZ's functionality, third-party services or applicable requirements change. Material updates will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
