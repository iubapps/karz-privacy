Privacy Policy

Vehicle management, with privacy in mind.

Effective date: 16 October 2026

KARZ is a vehicle management application developed by IUB Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use KARZ, including its optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup features.

1. Information You Provide

KARZ allows you to record vehicle information, maintenance and service history, fuel records, expenses, reminders, notes, and related details. You may also attach photographs, receipts, and PDF documents.

This information is entered and managed by you to provide the application's vehicle management features.

2. Local Data Storage

KARZ stores your vehicle management records on your device. The app uses local storage so you can access supported features without an internet connection.

Information stored locally remains subject to the security of your device, operating system, and any device-level backup settings.

3. Google Sign-In and Google Drive Backups

Google account connection is optional. If you choose to connect your Google account, KARZ uses Google authentication services to enable Google Drive backup and restore functionality.

When you initiate or enable a backup, KARZ may transfer your saved vehicle records and associated supported attachments to the application-specific storage area of your Google Drive account. Backup and restore operations require internet connectivity and are subject to Google's services and policies.

KARZ uses the Google account authorization you grant for this feature. Your Google account and backup information are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Photos, Receipts, and Documents

When you choose to attach images or documents, KARZ accesses the files you select to associate them with your vehicle records. Supported attachments may be included when you use the app's backup or export features.

If you share exported files with another application or person, their handling of those files is outside KARZ's control.

5. Notifications and Reminders

KARZ may use device notification permissions to provide vehicle maintenance and service reminders. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

6. Third-Party Services

KARZ uses Google services for optional account authentication and Google Drive backup functionality. These services may process technical information required to provide authentication, security, connectivity, and backup operations under their own privacy policies.

Device platform services may also process technical or diagnostic information as part of normal app installation, operation, and security.

7. Data Sharing

KARZ's vehicle management features are designed around information you store and manage yourself. Information may be transmitted to Google when you choose to use Google authentication or Drive backup features, or to destinations you select when sharing or exporting records.

KARZ does not require you to make your vehicle records publicly available to use its core tracking features.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

You can edit and delete vehicle records within KARZ. You can remove locally stored application data through the app's available controls or your device's application storage settings.

Uninstalling KARZ does not necessarily delete previously created external backups or exported files. Backup data stored in your Google account and files exported elsewhere may need to be managed separately.

9. Data Security

We aim to minimize unnecessary access to your information by keeping core vehicle records on your device and making Google account connection optional. However, no storage or data transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. We recommend protecting your device and Google account with appropriate security measures.

10. Children's Privacy

KARZ is a general vehicle management application and is not designed specifically for children. The application is intended for users managing vehicles and related expenses.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as KARZ's functionality or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.
